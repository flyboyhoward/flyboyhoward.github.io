Wenhao Wang — Academic Homepage

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science in the Department of Computer & Information Science at the University of Massachusetts Dartmouth, with an expected graduation date of May 2027.

My research explores how language models can enable reliable robot operation, with a focus on task planning, operation code generation, and autonomous UAV control. My recent work studies both large language models and small models that can run onboard robots.

My research interests include:

  • Language models for robotics: task planning and operation code generation.
  • Reliable autonomous systems: corrective feedback, static simulation, and closed-loop UAV operation.
  • Onboard intelligence: small language models and skill-driven robot operation.
