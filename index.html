WELCOME TO MY ACADEMIC HOMEPAGE

Hi, I’m Wenhao Wang.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science in the Department of Computer & Information Science at the University of Massachusetts Dartmouth.

My research explores how language models can enable reliable robot operation, with a focus on task planning, code generation, and autonomous UAV control. My recent work studies both large language models and small models that can run onboard robots.

Previously, I earned my M.Sc. in System, Control and Signal Processing from the University of Southampton and my B.Eng. in Automation from Shandong University.
